Team Solstice (our crew) task: Colonize the moon and create a base there for experiments and long-term human presence

    Easy aspects and potential difficulties of moon colonization:

  1. There is more than 600 billion kilograms of frozen ice at the poles of the Moon, which could be used as a efficient water supply.
  2. There was a 1967 Outer Space treaty which says that no country can claim any celestial object, including the moon, meaning countries that don't usually negotiate will have to work together.
  3. Temperature on the moon can swing 660 degrees fahrenheit between shadowy areas and sunsoaked canyons, making navigation very perilous.
  4. One advantage is that the the moon has 1/6th of the earth's gravity, significantly lowering costs of launching rockets and transporting cargo
