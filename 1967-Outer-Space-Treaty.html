The 1967 Outer Space treaty came from some proposals from 1959-1962 from Western powers, namely, Europe and the United States to ban the use of outer space for military purposes.

Then, in September 22nd, 1960, President Eisenhower proposed that the rules of the "Antarctic Treaty" be applied to Outer Space and any celestial body on it. The Antarctic Treaty, is a treaty that was enforced in 1961. It prohibits military use, and only allows scientists with permit to investigate the area, and that all the data will be shared between countries.
