The temperature on the moon changes unthinkably depending if your in a sunny plain or in a dark, intrenched canyon. Temperatures can reach 253 degrees Fahrenheit in the day, which is hotter than boiling water, and dip to a "-243 degrees Fahrenheit at night." In areas that the sun's light can't reach, namely the Permanent Shadowed crater, the temperature might drop as low as -413 degrees Fahrenheit. That’s means of the temperature might swing 660 degrees apart! The hottest temperature on Earth ever recorded was 136 degrees Fahrenheit in the Libyan desert, and the coldest was "-126 degrees Fahrenheit at the Vostok Station in Antarctica." That’s a span of 262 degrees, which is about 3 times less than the temperature swings on the lunar surface.
